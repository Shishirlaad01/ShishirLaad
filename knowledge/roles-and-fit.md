# Role fit: the evidence for each kind of role

Shishir is open to three kinds of roles. For each, here is the experience that backs it up. Job titles below are his actual titles; where he did the work of a role under a different title, that is said plainly.

## Project, Program, or Delivery Manager
- 15+ years in delivery; titled Project Manager at every employer since late 2016, and has also worked as Program Manager, Project Lead, Team Lead, and Project Coordinator.
- Has run large and parallel delivery: a 74-member Scrum organization for a US healthcare program, 4 BAs and 55 developers at HyperBeans, a 45-member distributed organization across 5+ concurrent projects at HiddenBrains, and a 25-member support and delivery team.
- Owns the full lifecycle: requirements, proposals, estimation, planning, risk and dependency management, releases and environments, UAT, and go-live.
- Delivery governance and reporting: SDLC and Agile governance, KPI tracking, burn-down and burn-up, Gantt charts, budget control, and status reporting to leadership and clients.
- International and government stakeholders: clients in the UK, USA, New Zealand, Germany, the Netherlands, the UAE, and South Africa, and 3+ government departments in India.
- Business result example: a UK insurance platform he delivered end to end cut manual processing time by 40% and improved policy turnaround by 25%.
- AI delivery: initiated and scaled the AI practice at HiddenBrains from 4 to 30+ engineers and enabled 12-15 projects delivered with AI over the past year.
- Credentials: Executive Certification in Project Management from IIM Kashipur.

## Scrum Master or Agile Project Manager
- Certified Scrum Master (CSM, Scrum Alliance, 2020).
- Acted as Scrum Master for cross-functional Scrum teams of 74 members on an enterprise US healthcare program, and for the India development team of a New Zealand FinTech product.
- Facilitates the full set of ceremonies: sprint planning, daily stand-ups, sprint reviews and demos, retrospectives, backlog refinement, and release planning.
- Agile practice: Definition of Ready and Done, impediment removal, dependency and risk management, multi-team release coordination, and Kanban.
- Metrics: velocity, sprint burn-down and burn-up, sprint goal tracking, release readiness, and Kanban flow.
- Coaching: has led Agile transformation initiatives, run client workshops, and mentored cross-functional teams; trained 40+ developers in AI-assisted practices.
- Tools: Jira, Confluence, Trello, Basecamp, Redmine, Zoho Projects.

## Product Owner or Product Manager
- Has held the Product Owner role in his career, and does product ownership work as part of his project manager roles.
- FinTech (New Zealand): shaped and prioritized the roadmap and backlog of an AR/AP automation product (epics, user stories, acceptance criteria) using market research, competitor analysis, and sales and prospect input. Introduced AI-based invoice capture (ABBYY Vantage OCR). Worked as the organization's bookkeeper to learn the domain hands-on, including Xero and MYOB integrations and workflows, to guide product decisions.
- AI products: at HiddenBrains he spotted a documentation and requirements bottleneck slowing development teams, then designed and shipped a suite of 7 custom GPTs that cut requirement and documentation turnaround by about 20-30%, and drove their adoption.
- Discovery and requirements: owns client discovery and requirements across 5+ concurrent products at HiddenBrains; writes BRDs, FRDs, PRDs, and SRS documents; produces wireframes and prototypes in Figma and Axure.
- Business analysis foundation: worked as a Senior Business Analyst, and has led BA teams for years.
- Healthcare: led delivery for a US healthcare research platform on web, iOS, and Android built to HIPAA and GDPR requirements, and later led the vendor-side team for a US healthcare product, translating client needs into delivery priorities.
- Domain range: FinTech, Healthcare, InsurTech, E-Governance, B2B SaaS, E-commerce, marketplaces.
