# Custom GPTs

Generative AI tools Shishir has designed and uses in real delivery work. He built a suite of 7; the six below are free to try in ChatGPT, from the AI Innovation section of this site or at the links below. The seventh, Figma to MD File Generator, converts Figma design files into structured markdown documentation and is not listed on this site.

## Multi-Agent Prompt Creator
Designs structured multi-agent prompt workflows. Useful for anyone designing AI workflows or complex prompts.
Try it: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-69c66bcd86b08191bfe754510ea1f30d-prompt-creator-shishir

## Meeting Minutes Specialist
Generates structured minutes of meeting (MoM) from meeting transcripts. Useful for project managers, Scrum Masters, and anyone who runs client or team meetings.
Try it: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-69254f1b026481918fd423d3e974522e-meeting-minutes-specialist

## Scope & Proposal Creator
Generates client-facing scope documents and proposals. Useful for presales, business analysts, and delivery managers preparing proposals.
Try it: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-69274b4db5c0819187dfcd4615551bbe-hb-presales-master-ba

## Document Formatter
Standardizes and formats project documentation.
Try it: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-69b294d0aea8819189dd6218d41faf28-document-formatter

## FRD Creator (Vibe Coding)
Generates functional requirement documents (FRDs) optimized for AI-assisted ("vibe") coding workflows. Useful for product owners, business analysts, and teams building with AI coding tools.
Try it: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6985c6576e4081918191ac8785900616-frd-creation-rnd

## FRD to MD Generator
Converts FRD documents into developer-ready markdown.
Try it: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-695ea049a8a0819196f396b6c27b712b-hb-frd-to-md-file-generator
